# Instructor notes — ETL capstone

Ask learners to avoid opening the sample defect report until after they have written their own investigation. The goal is not to memorize the expected $15 discrepancy; it is to gather and classify evidence.

**10-point rubric:** independent reconciliation (2); separate defect mechanisms (2); observation versus inference (2); risk and release decision (2); remediation plus retest criteria (2).

**Important nuance:** Because the capstone does not include the faulty original SQL, the duplicate dimension supports a strong *hypothesis* for the repeated sales fact but does not prove its code-level origin. Encourage precision in root-cause claims.

Lab 05 concludes the bounded initial curriculum. Future presentation enhancements should not rewrite or weaken the accepted tests from Labs 01–04.
