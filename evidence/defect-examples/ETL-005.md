# ETL-005 — Paid order amount differs from approved expectation

**Classification:** Synthetic teaching defect
**Severity:** High (business revenue overstatement)
**Status:** Reproduced by controlled fixture

## Reproduction
Run `python -m scripts.check --dataset defective`.

## Expected vs actual
- Expected paid-order count: 3; actual count: 3.
- Expected revenue: $120.00; actual revenue: $132.50.
- Order `O-1002`: expected $60.10; actual $72.60 (difference +$12.50).
- The job completes successfully; count-only validation misses the defect.

## Analysis and disposition
- **Observed cause in this fixture:** altered `unit_price` for `O-1002`.
- **Potential business impact:** overstated sales reporting despite correct record counts.
- **Disposition:** Hold publication for the affected output; establish source authority, correct input upstream, rerun and retain reconciliation evidence. Do not just adjust the aggregate or accept the load status.
